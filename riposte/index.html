Make them commit

Slash, stab or strike overhead. Feint into another attack, reverse your swing and punish a mistimed defense.

Turn defense into damage

Parry, answer with a counter, or kick to break a held guard. Watch your stamina: every exchange has a cost.

Climb, or settle a score

Find an opponent automatically and climb the Elo ranks. Send a private lobby link to a friend for a duel without ranked rewards.

Before you draw your sword

Is RIPOSTE free to play?

Yes. Open the game in a desktop browser and play. Cosmetic rewards are earned through play.

Do I need an account?

You can try the arena as a guest. Create an account to keep your Elo rank, statistics and unlocked equipment.

Can I play against a friend?

Yes. Create a private lobby and share its link. Private duels do not count toward rank or progression.

How do I learn the controls?

An old trainer introduces movement, attacks and defense in a short practice session. You can replay training from the lobby.
