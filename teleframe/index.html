A frame you can put anywhere

Pick a shape from the control strip: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, or 1:1, 4:3, 4:5, 21:9, a free shape or the whole monitor.

Drag a corner to resize and the ratio holds. You can move the frame while you record, and the video follows it.

Pause, cut the bad take, save

Every stretch between REC and pause becomes a clip. Fluffed a line? Delete the last clip and record it again.

When you press stop, Teleframe joins the clips into one MP4 in a few seconds. It copies the video as it is, so nothing is re-encoded.

The controls sit on one thin line under the frame. Collapse them to a single button when you want a clean screen.

The recorder stays out of the video

Windows can hide a window from screen capture. Teleframe turns that on for all of its own windows: the frame, the control strip and the settings. Record the full screen with the controls on top and they still won't be in the file.

Your mouse pointer is recorded as usual.

Questions

Is Teleframe a subscription?

No. You pay once, and the copy you download is yours to keep. There's no account to make.

Does Teleframe add a watermark?

No. The video contains what was inside the frame and nothing else.

Can it record sound?

Yes. In settings, choose system sound, your microphone, or both mixed together. Sound is saved as AAC at 192 kbps, about 1.4 MB per minute.

Can I record vertical video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts?

Yes. Choose 9:16 and the frame turns into a tall rectangle. Put it over the part of the screen you want and press REC.

Where are my recordings saved?

In the folder you pick in settings, which is Videos\Teleframe until you change it. Each recording is an MP4 named with the date and time.

How big are the files?

That depends on the frame size and the quality setting. At HIGH and 60 fps, a minute of 1080p video is about 113 MB. Settings shows the estimate for your frame before you start.

Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?

The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time you run it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does Teleframe work on a Mac?

No. Teleframe runs on 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) and Windows 11.
